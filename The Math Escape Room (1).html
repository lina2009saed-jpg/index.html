The Math Escape Room

You’re locked in! Divide fractions to find the keys and escape.

How to play

  1. There are 3 rooms, and every room has locked doors to open.
  2. Tap the answer you think is right. You find out straight away.
  3. Each correct answer is 10 points. Get every puzzle in a room right for a +5 bonus.
  4. Read the explanation after each puzzle. It helps with the next one!
Secret trick: Keep, Change, Flip.
Keep the first fraction, change ÷ to ×, then flip the second fraction upside down. Multiply and simplify.
